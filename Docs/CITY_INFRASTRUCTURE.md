**Global Scale:** 7 Major Metropolises  
**Economic Model:** Dominion Economy with City-Specific Sub-Models

---

## 🌍 Overview

The OmniWorld metaverse is sharded into **7 Global Metropolises**, each representing a distinct cultural, economic, and social ecosystem. While all cities feed into the unified **Dominion Economy**, each operates with unique characteristics that create arbitrage opportunities, specialization incentives, and diverse user experiences.

### Core Principle

**"Every city is a sovereign economic zone with distinct advantages, but all participate in the global market."**

This creates:
- **Arbitrage opportunities** between city markets
- **Specialization incentives** for businesses and creators
- **Migration patterns** based on opportunity
- **Cultural identity** and community formation
- **Economic diversity** preventing mono-culture
