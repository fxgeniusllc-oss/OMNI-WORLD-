## Visual Design Guidelines

### Render Requirements

**Boxing Gym:**
- Warm, nostalgic lighting
- Worn leather textures
- Vintage poster materials
- Concrete and rubber floor mixing
- Chain-link fence (speed bags)
- Red accent lighting

**MMA Center:**
- Modern, clean aesthetic
- High-contrast lighting
- Digital displays
- Reflective surfaces (mirrors)
- LED accent strips
- Professional grade equipment

**Street Arena:**
- Dark, gritty atmosphere
- Heavy weathering on all surfaces
- Graffiti textures (hand-painted look)
- Rust and blood stains
- Dramatic shadows
- Red neon glow

### Material Specifications

**Common Materials:**
- Concrete (various weathering levels)
- Steel (painted, rusty, polished)
- Leather (new to worn spectrum)
- Canvas (boxing ring, octagon)
- Rubber (mats, flooring)
- Chain-link fence

**Texture Resolutions:**
- Diffuse: 2048x2048 minimum
- Normal maps: 2048x2048
- Roughness: 1024x1024
- AO (Ambient Occlusion): 1024x1024

### LOD (Level of Detail) Strategy

**LOD0 (Full Detail):**
- Full geometry
- All textures at max resolution
- Used within 10 meters

**LOD1:**
- 50% poly reduction
- Half-resolution textures
- 10-25 meters

**LOD2:**
- 75% poly reduction
- Quarter-resolution textures
- 25-50 meters

**LOD3 (Billboard):**
- Simple planes
- Single texture atlas
- Beyond 50 meters
